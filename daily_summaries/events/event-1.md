# NRO to expand use of HawkEye 360’s satellite intelligence

SpaceNews reports the National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) will expand use of HawkEye 360’s satellite intelligence under a contract that increases U.S. government access to HawkEye 360 data used to locate radars, ships and other electronic emitters.

## Industry implication

An expanded NRO contract would validate and increase government demand for commercial RF geolocation and satellite-intelligence services, likely benefiting Hawkeye 360 Inc through greater government access to its data and potentially making similar commercial providers more competitive for government work.

## Key facts

- SpaceNews reports the NRO will expand use of HawkEye 360's satellite intelligence.
- The contract increases U.S. government access to HawkEye 360 satellite data.
- The data is used to locate radars, ships and other electronic emitters.

## Risks

- Article summary does not specify the contract size or duration; the commercial impact may be limited if the contract is small or short-term.
- Dependence on a single large government customer could concentrate revenue and operational risk for Hawkeye 360 Inc.
- Classified use or restrictive handling requirements could limit the ability to commercialize related capabilities.
- Competitive responses from other commercial imagery/signals providers or changes in government procurement priorities could reduce expected benefits.
