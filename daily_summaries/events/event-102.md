# Voyager Technologies’ Griffin-1 Completes Environmental Testing at NASA JPL

Voyager Technologies’ Griffin-1 lunar lander completed nearly three months of environmental and vibration testing at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Southern California, with testing finishing in late September 2026 (reported as 2026-09-30). The tests — which simulated extreme temperatures and the vacuum of space and included intense vibration/shaking and high (described as "ear-splitting") noise — were reported to demonstrate the spacecraft’s preparedness for launch, transit to the Moon, and landing operations.

## Industry implication

Completion of JPL environmental and vibration testing is a positive technology-readiness signal for Voyager’s Griffin-1 program and could increase partner and customer confidence and strengthen Voyager’s position in upcoming lunar missions and potential NASA commercial partnerships (including Moon Base efforts). However, this milestone does not guarantee a launch date, mission approval, or mission success; further integration, qualification, funding, launch vehicle selection, and program milestones remain.

- Griffin-1 is a lunar lander from Voyager Technologies.
