# UARX Space troubleshoots anomalies on maiden OTV Flight

Spanish startup UARX Space is troubleshooting anomalies discovered during the maiden flight of its OSSIE-D orbital transfer vehicle, which was launched Oct. 1 on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare mission.

## Industry implication

Anomalies on a maiden OTV flight could slow commercialization and customer uptake of new orbital transfer services, raise questions about OTV reliability for rideshare customers, and increase near-term scrutiny of similar small startup missions.

## Key facts

- UARX Space identified anomalies during the maiden flight of OSSIE-D, an orbital transfer vehicle (OTV).
- OSSIE-D was launched Oct. 1 aboard SpaceX’s Transporter-18 mission.
- UARX Space is remedying/troubleshooting the anomalies (per the article).

## Risks

- Potential mission degradation or loss of OSSIE-D functionality if anomalies are not resolved.
- Reduced customer or investor confidence in UARX Space and similar OTV startups following a troubled maiden flight.
- Possible increased attention from customers, insurers, or regulators on OTV testing and qualification practices.
