# Solstar provides Wi-Fi connectivity for NASA proximity operations demonstration

Solstar Space's Wi‑Fi connected NASA’s R5‑S10 cubesat with Momentus’ Vigoride‑7 during a proximity-operations demonstration and relayed data to NASA’s Johnson Space Center.

## Industry implication

A successful in-space Wi‑Fi link between a cubesat and an orbital-servicing vehicle demonstrates an alternative communications approach for proximity operations and data relay, which could lower complexity or cost of relative‑navigation and servicing demonstrations and support future orbital servicing activities if broadly validated.

## Key facts

- Solstar Space provided Wi‑Fi technology that connected NASA’s R5‑S10 cubesat with Momentus’ Vigoride‑7 orbital service vehicle.
- The Wi‑Fi link was used during proximity operations and relayed data to NASA’s Johnson Space Center.
- The activity is described as a NASA proximity operations demonstration.

## Risks

- Summary does not state whether the demonstration was continuous, long‑duration, or limited to specific maneuvers; results may not generalize to all proximity‑operations scenarios.
- Solstar Space and NASA operational details (e.g., security, throughput, latency) are not provided, so capability and reliability of the Wi‑Fi link are uncertain.
- This is a demonstration; additional testing and integration work would be needed before commercial or operational adoption by orbital‑servicing providers.
