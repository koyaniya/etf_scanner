# Astranis Upgrades Falcon 9 Launch Contract to Dedicated Mission for Entire Block 4 Production Run of Satellites

On Oct. 26, 2026 Astranis announced it upgraded its 4th launch to a dedicated mission aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket (Space Exploration Technologies Corp), scheduled to launch in 2028, covering the entire Block 4 production run of satellites.

## Industry implication

A dedicated Falcon 9 mission for an entire production run increases schedule certainty for the satellite builder and represents a larger commercial commitment to SpaceX's launch services. This could accelerate Astranis's deployment timeline for the Block 4 run and modestly increase Falcon 9 manifest utilization in the medium term. Relying on a single dedicated mission also concentrates launch risk on one provider/vehicle.

## Key facts

- Astranis announced an upgrade of its 4th launch to a dedicated mission aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket.
- The announcement date is Oct. 26, 2026.
- The Falcon 9 launch is set to occur in 2028.
- The upgrade covers the entire Block 4 production run of satellites.

## Risks

- Concentrating a production run on a single dedicated launch increases exposure to schedule slips or anomalies affecting that launch.
- Planned 2028 launch date could slip due to vehicle, manifest, or regulatory delays.
- Dependence on a single launch provider may limit flexibility if changes to satellite availability or requirements occur.
