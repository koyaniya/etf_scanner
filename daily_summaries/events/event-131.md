# Flexell Space and Satoro Agree to Collaborate on In-Orbit Demonstration of Next-Generation Space Solar Cells

Flexell Space and Satoro announced a collaboration to perform in-orbit demonstrations of next-generation space solar cells, with two demonstration missions planned aboard SpaceX Falcon 9 — one in 2027 and one in 2028. Satoro will provide a "Satellite-as-a-service" solution and Flexell Space will supply the solar cells and test modules.

## Industry implication

If successful, these in-orbit demonstrations could accelerate adoption of next-generation solar cell technologies on commercial satellites, validate satellite-as-a-service deployment models, and generate additional Falcon 9 payload demand for the launch market. Demonstrations may also influence satellite power-system design choices and supplier relationships across small- and medium-satellite manufacturers.

## Key facts

- Flexell Space and Satoro agreed to collaborate on in-orbit demonstration of next-generation space solar cells.
- Two demonstration missions are planned aboard SpaceX Falcon 9: one in 2027 and one in 2028.
- Satoro will provide a "Satellite-as-a-service" solution for the demonstrations.
- Flexell Space will supply the solar cells and test modules for the demonstrations.

## Risks

- Launch schedule risk or manifest changes for the Falcon 9 missions could delay demonstrations.
- Technical risk that the solar cell technology or test modules may not perform as expected in orbit.
- Limited information in the summary about mission scope and contractual terms increases uncertainty about commercial or technical outcomes.
- Dependence on a single launch provider for both demonstrations concentrates schedule and execution risk.
