# Elve qualifies millimeter-wave amplifiers for spaceflight

California startup Elve qualified its 100-watt millimeter-wave Traveling Wave Tube Amplifier (TWT) platform for spaceflight; the effort was funded by a U.S. Space Force Small Business Innovation Research award.

## Industry implication

If adopted, a spaceflight-qualified 100 W millimeter-wave TWT could enable higher-power millimeter-wave satellite links for defense and other users and expand supplier options for high-frequency space communications; impact depends on Elve's ability to scale production and on customer adoption within military and commercial programs.

## Key facts

- Elve is a California startup.
- Elve qualified a 100-watt millimeter-wave Traveling Wave Tube Amplifier platform for spaceflight.
- The qualification effort was supported by U.S. Space Force Small Business Innovation Research funding.
