# Space Force awards five companies $60 million to prototype multi-vendor connections to SpaceX-built network

The U.S. Space Force awarded five companies a total of $60 million to prototype and test multi-vendor connections to a SpaceX-built data network backbone. The named awardees in the reporting are Amazon Leo, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Rocket Lab and York Space (SpaceNews, published 2026-08-13).

## Industry implication

The award supports movement toward multi-vendor, interoperable satellite communications architectures for defense use. Successful prototyping and testing could reduce barriers to integrating non-SpaceX systems with the SpaceX-built backbone, encouraging broader commercial participation in government satellite networking and potentially accelerating adoption of multi-vendor approaches in DoD space communications.
