# NASA Invites Media, Public to Join Voyager 1 One-Light Day Activities

NASA will host public and media activities on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026 to mark Voyager 1 reaching a distance of one light-day from Earth (about 16+ billion miles / 25.9 billion km). The milestone highlights that light from Earth takes ~24 hours to reach the spacecraft; NASA is inviting the public and media to join related events.

## Industry implication

Primarily a public-outreach and milestone event that may increase public and scientific attention on long-duration, deep-space missions. It does not indicate operational changes or commercial contracts and is unlikely to have significant direct commercial impact.

## Key facts

- NASA invited media and the public to join activities marking Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth.
- The summary states Voyager 1 was launched in 1977 and on Wednesday, Nov. 18 will have traveled one light-day from Earth.
- The one light-day distance is described as more than 16 billion miles (25.9 billion kilometers) and implies ~24 hours light-travel time from Earth to the spacecraft.
- Voyager 1 is described as the most distant human-made object.
