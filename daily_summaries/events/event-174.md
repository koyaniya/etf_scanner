# AST SpaceMobile presses case for developing own launch vehicle

AST SpaceMobile executives publicly stated the company believes it can develop and field a new reusable launch vehicle within two years, a claim made despite reported industry skepticism.

## Industry implication

If AST SpaceMobile pursues development of its own reusable launch vehicle, it could alter its reliance on external launch providers and introduce a new competitor or demand driver in the small-to-medium launch market. The plan also creates execution risk for AST SpaceMobile (technical, regulatory and financing) and could distract from its core satellite communications business if the company diverts resources to vehicle development.

## Key facts

- AST SpaceMobile executives expressed confidence in the company’s ability to field a new reusable launch vehicle in just two years (as reported in the article).
- The article notes there is widespread industry skepticism about that claim.
- The report about the executives' statements appeared on SpaceNews (article published 2026-11-13).

## Risks

- AST SpaceMobile may face significant technical and schedule risk developing a reusable launch vehicle within a two-year timeline.
- Potential funding and resource diversion from AST SpaceMobile’s core satellite business could occur if the company commits to vehicle development.
- Regulatory approvals, certification, and supply-chain constraints could delay or complicate the proposed timeline.
