# NICER Status Updates

NASA's NICER (Neutron star Interior Composition Explorer) on the International Space Station ended science operations on November 17, 2026; mission and ISS teams are shifting focus to plans to finalize NICER's 8-year mission.

## Industry implication

The end of NICER's science operations removes an active X-ray astrophysics instrument on the ISS, reducing available space-based X-ray timing/observational capacity and triggering decommissioning activities that will occupy mission and station resources in the near term.

## Key facts

- On November 17, 2026, NASA’s NICER telescope on the International Space Station ended science operations.
- NICER had studied the universe for six and a half years beyond its initial mission.
- Mission and space station teams are now focused on plans to finalize NICER’s 8-year mission.

## Risks

- Loss of ongoing science observations and potential gap in X-ray timing/astrophysics data coverage following NICER's cessation.
- Operational and technical risks associated with finalizing and decommissioning an instrument on the ISS.
- Potential short-term impacts on research programs that relied on NICER data while transition plans are implemented.
