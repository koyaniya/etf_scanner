# NASA Sets Coverage for Northrop Grumman CRS-25 Launch, Prelaunch

NASA, Northrop Grumman, and SpaceX announced a targeted launch time of no earlier than 10:47 a.m. EST on Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026 for the Northrop Grumman CRS-25 Cygnus resupply mission to the International Space Station; a prelaunch media teleconference is scheduled for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.

## Industry implication

Scheduling of the CRS-25 launch maintains the ISS resupply cadence and represents near-term operational activity for Northrop Grumman under its cargo resupply missions and for SpaceX as the cited launch partner, which is positive for both companies' launch and service operations in the short term.

## Key facts

- NASA, Northrop Grumman, and SpaceX are targeting no earlier than 10:47 a.m. EST on Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026 for the CRS-25 launch.
- A prelaunch media teleconference is scheduled for Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.
- The Northrop Grumman Cygnus is filled with approximately 11,000 pounds of cargo to deliver science investigations, supplies, and equipment to the International Space Station.

## Risks

- Scheduled launch date/time is a target and may change (subject to weather, technical issues, or range availability).
- Prelaunch activities (e.g., vehicle processing, payload readiness) could introduce delays or schedule slips.
