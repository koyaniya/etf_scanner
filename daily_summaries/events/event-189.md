# Empire State Building Goes Gold for Voyager

The Empire State Building was illuminated in gold on Nov. 17, 2026 to celebrate NASA’s Voyager 1 spacecraft as it was about to pass one light‑day away from Earth. The gold lighting references the gold‑plated copper phonograph (the Golden Record) aboard Voyager.

## Industry implication

Primarily a public outreach and commemorative event that may raise public awareness and interest in deep‑space missions; it does not indicate operational, contractual, or technical changes for tracked space companies.

## Key facts

- The Empire State Building was illuminated in gold on Nov. 17, 2026.
- The illumination was to celebrate NASA’s Voyager 1 spacecraft as it was about to pass one light‑day away from Earth.
- The color gold represents the gold‑plated copper phonograph record (the Golden Record) aboard Voyager.
