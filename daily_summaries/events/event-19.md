# First Neutron launch may slip to 2027

Rocket Lab warned that the probability of its Neutron rocket making a first flight before the end of the year is decreasing and said the first Neutron launch may slip to 2027, despite progress on the vehicle’s development (source: SpaceNews).

## Industry implication

A possible slip of Neutron's maiden flight to 2027 represents schedule risk for Rocket Lab's entry into larger-launch market capacity and could delay planned payload missions or revenue tied to Neutron. The delay may affect customers' launch timelines and competitive dynamics in the medium term and could raise short-term investor and customer concerns about Rocket Lab's execution of a next-generation vehicle.

## Key facts

- Rocket Lab warned that the chances of launching its Neutron rocket for the first time before the end of the year are "narrowing."
- The article states the first Neutron launch may slip to 2027.
- The article notes progress on the vehicle's development despite the warning.
- Source: SpaceNews (article titled "First Neutron launch may slip to 2027").

## Risks

- Schedule slip of Neutron maiden flight into 2027 (as reported).
- Potential impacts to customers waiting for Neutron-class launch capacity.
- Reputational and program execution risk for Rocket Lab if the delay occurs.
