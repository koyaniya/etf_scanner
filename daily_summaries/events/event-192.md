# NASA Awards Contract for NOAA’s Near Earth Orbit Network Program

NASA, on behalf of NOAA, awarded a $290.6 million hybrid contract to Northrop Grumman Systems Corporation to provide Sounder for Microwave-Based Applications (SMBA) instruments for NOAA’s Near Earth Orbit Network (NEON) satellites.

## Industry implication

The award directs a sizable near-term revenue stream and engineering workload to Northrop Grumman and signals continued U.S. government investment in space-based environmental sensing for low-Earth orbit platforms. It may influence supplier demand and competitive positioning among prime contractors for NOAA satellite instrument work.

## Key facts

- NASA, on behalf of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), awarded a contract to Northrop Grumman Systems Corporation.
- The contract is to deliver Sounder for Microwave-Based Applications (SMBA) instruments for NOAA’s Near Earth Orbit Network (NEON) satellites.
- The contract is described as a hybrid of cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price, and time-and-materials contract types.
- The contract value is $290.6 million.
- The summary states there is a period of performance but does not specify dates in the provided text.

## Risks

- Program schedule slips or technical challenges developing the SMBA instruments could delay deliveries or increase costs.
- Changes in NOAA or federal funding/priorities could affect scope or continuation of work.
- Supply-chain constraints for specialized components could impact performance or cost under the hybrid contract.
