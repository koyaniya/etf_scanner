# NASA Enhances Supplier Program, Announces Mentor-Protégé Agreement

NASA announced a Mentor‑Protégé Agreement under its recently renamed Small Business Supplier Development Program between The Boeing Company and GRACE, part of an effort to strengthen the domestic aerospace industrial base to support future exploration missions.

## Industry implication

The agreement signals continued NASA emphasis on integrating large primes with small suppliers to shore up the U.S. aerospace supply chain. For Boeing this could improve access to vetted small‑business suppliers and support its positioning on future NASA exploration contracts; for the sector it may help strengthen domestic supplier capabilities over the medium term.

## Key facts

- NASA is evolving how it works with small businesses and is strengthening the domestic aerospace industrial base through its recently renamed Small Business Supplier Development Program.
- As part of that program, NASA announced its latest Mentor‑Protégé Agreement signed between The Boeing Company and GRACE.
- The program is intended to support future exploration missions.

## Risks

- Summary lacks details on the agreement’s scope, duration, and monetary value, limiting assessment of direct commercial impact on Boeing.
- Mentor‑protégé agreements do not guarantee future contract awards or long‑term supplier performance improvements.
- Program renaming and announcements do not ensure continued funding or successful implementation across the supply chain.
