# New report takes closer look at the Space Force spending surge

Aerospace Corp. published a new analysis reporting a surge in Space Force spending, with most growth concentrated in classified programs, target tracking, missile warning and command-and-control capabilities, according to a SpaceNews summary of the report.

## Industry implication

If the Aerospace Corp. analysis is accurate, increased Space Force spending concentrated in those areas would favor contractors and suppliers that provide classified systems, missile warning, target-tracking sensors and command-and-control solutions, and could shift program priorities and procurement toward those capabilities in the near term.

## Key facts

- SpaceNews reports that Aerospace Corp. produced an analysis examining a Space Force spending surge.
- The Aerospace Corp. analysis finds most growth concentrated in classified programs, target tracking, missile warning and command and control.

## Risks

- Summary does not provide the report’s publication date, data, or methodology, limiting verification of the findings.
- The report describes concentrations of spending but does not identify specific contracts, dollar amounts, or named contractors, so downstream commercial impacts are uncertain.
- Classified-program concentration reduces transparency and may delay market signals to commercial suppliers about procurement opportunities.
