# Rocket Lab builds deployable infrastructure for Electron launches

Rocket Lab has built a containerized, deployable infrastructure named 'GHOST' designed to support Electron orbital and suborbital launches.

## Industry implication

If deployed, GHOST could allow Rocket Lab to operate launches with less dependence on permanent ground infrastructure, increasing operational flexibility and potentially enabling faster or more remote launch operations — improving its competitive position in small-launch services. The actual operational and commercial impact will depend on deployment pace, regulatory approvals, and integration with existing launch processes.

## Key facts

- Rocket Lab built a containerized, deployable infrastructure named 'GHOST'.
- GHOST is designed to support orbital and suborbital launches.
- The infrastructure is intended for use with Electron launches (as stated in the article title).

## Risks

- Deployment and operation may face logistical and integration challenges with existing launch procedures and sites.
- Use of deployable launch infrastructure could require regulatory approvals or site-specific permissions that delay operational use.
- Costs, maintenance needs, or technical issues with a new infrastructure concept could limit near-term benefits.
