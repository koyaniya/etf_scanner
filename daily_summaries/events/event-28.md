# Voyager seeks relaxed requirements in NASA commercial space station RFP

Voyager Technologies asked for relaxed requirements in NASA’s commercial space station RFP and said it remains upbeat about prospects for its Starlab commercial space station, seeking tweaks to NASA’s plans to support the station’s development.

## Industry implication

If NASA relaxes RFP requirements, Voyager could find it easier to meet procurement terms and advance Starlab development, potentially improving the company's near-term prospects in the commercial space station market. Changes to RFP requirements could also affect competition and NASA’s oversight standards for commercial low‑Earth-orbit platforms.
