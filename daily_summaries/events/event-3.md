# Hypersonic missile startup Castelion raises $1 billion

Hypersonic missile startup Castelion has raised $1 billion. The company, founded by former SpaceX executives, aims to produce a low-cost hypersonic weapon and a broader portfolio of longer-range strike and missile defense weapons.

## Industry implication

A large private funding round for a hypersonics-focused startup could accelerate private-sector development of advanced strike and missile-defense technologies, attract talent and suppliers, and increase competition for established defense contractors and primes in hypersonics-related work. It may also draw greater government and regulatory attention to commercialization of weapon-related space and propulsion capabilities.

## Key facts

- Castelion raised $1 billion (as reported).
- Castelion was founded by former SpaceX executives (as reported).
- Castelion aims to produce a low-cost hypersonic weapon and a broader portfolio of longer-range strike and missile defense weapons (as reported).

## Risks

- Significant technical and development challenges inherent in hypersonic weapons programs could delay or increase the cost of achieving operational capability.
- Potential regulatory, export-control, or national-security review could constrain certain activities or partnerships.
- Strong competition from established defense contractors and other startups may limit market opportunities or require additional capital beyond this round.
