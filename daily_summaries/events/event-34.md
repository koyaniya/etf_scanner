# Telesat lands $1.63 billion Canadian defense contract

SpaceNews reports that Telesat landed a $1.63 billion Canadian defense contract to fund an expansion of its Lightspeed LEO network, increasing the planned constellation to 225 spacecraft.

## Industry implication

The contract materially strengthens Telesat's position in defense-focused LEO satellite communications and accelerates expansion of its Lightspeed constellation, signaling increased government investment in LEO satcom capacity for Arctic/defense use. This could shift competitive dynamics in government satcom procurement and capacity available for national security applications.

## Key facts

- The article headline states Telesat landed a $1.63 billion Canadian defense contract.
- The award will fund an expansion of Telesat's Lightspeed LEO network.
- The planned Lightspeed constellation will be taken to 225 spacecraft per the article summary.

## Risks

- Execution risk in building and launching the additional satellites on schedule.
- Technical integration and performance risk in expanding the Lightspeed network.
- Potential for changes in government priorities or funding that could affect contract scope or timing.
- Supply-chain or launch availability constraints that could delay deployment.
