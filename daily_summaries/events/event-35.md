# Kreios Space to fly VLEO demonstration in NanoAvionics bus

Kreios Space announced on Aug. 4, 2026 plans to conduct the first very low Earth orbit (VLEO) demonstration of its air‑breathing electric propulsion (ABEP) system flown in a NanoAvionics satellite bus.

## Industry implication

If carried out successfully, the demonstration could validate air‑breathing electric propulsion for VLEO smallsats, potentially enabling longer missions at lower altitudes and influencing smallsat propulsion and bus design choices for VLEO operations.

## Key facts

- Spanish startup Kreios Space announced plans on Aug. 4, 2026 to conduct the first very low Earth orbit (VLEO) demonstration of its air‑breathing electric propulsion (ABEP).
- The planned demonstration will be flown in a NanoAvionics satellite bus (per the article title).

## Risks

- Announcement is of planned demonstration; schedule and execution details (launch date, integration partner confirmation) are not provided and may change.
- Technical risk: ABEP is experimental in VLEO and may face development or operational challenges in the high‑drag VLEO environment.
- Regulatory or launch provider constraints and potential integration issues with the NanoAvionics bus could delay or alter the demonstration.
