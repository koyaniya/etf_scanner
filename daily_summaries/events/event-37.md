# NASA selects successor to CAPSTONE lunar mission

NASA has selected Advanced Space to fly a successor to a cislunar smallsat mission that will test rendezvous and proximity operations around the moon.

## Industry implication

A NASA selection to fly a CAPSTONE successor signals continued government support for cislunar smallsat demonstrations and for technologies enabling rendezvous and proximity operations around the Moon. This could accelerate development and procurement of proximity-op and navigation/relative‑navigation systems, and increase opportunities for suppliers that support smallsat lunar operations.

## Key facts

- NASA selected Advanced Space to fly a successor to the CAPSTONE lunar mission.
- The successor is a cislunar smallsat mission.
- The mission will test rendezvous and proximity operations around the Moon.

## Risks

- Selection does not guarantee contract award or final scope; plans may change during procurement.
- Technical challenges inherent in testing rendezvous and proximity operations in cislunar space could cause schedule slips or mission changes.
- Launch and mission execution risks could affect demonstration outcomes and subsequent follow-on activity.
