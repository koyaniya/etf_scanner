# Boeing still working with NASA to schedule next Starliner flight

Boeing is working with NASA to determine the timing for the next flight of its CST-100 Starliner commercial crew vehicle, which would fly as a cargo mission to the International Space Station.

## Industry implication

The scheduling uncertainty means NASA and Boeing have not finalized when Starliner will next support ISS cargo operations; this could affect near-term cargo planning and Boeing's commercial crew program milestone tracking, but the summary does not indicate a firm delay or cancellation.

## Key facts

- Boeing is still working with NASA to determine when to fly its CST-100 Starliner commercial crew vehicle on a cargo mission to the International Space Station.

## Risks

- Timing for the next Starliner cargo flight is unresolved, creating uncertainty for NASA cargo scheduling and Boeing operational planning.
- Uncertainty in scheduling could lead to downstream impacts on ISS resupply manifests or reliance on alternate cargo providers if delays occur.
