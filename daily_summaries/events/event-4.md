# Firefly Aerospace to fly Zeno Power radioisotope heating unit on lunar lander mission

Firefly Aerospace will carry a Zeno Power radioisotope heating unit as a payload on an upcoming lunar lander mission to test a radioisotope heat source intended to help future missions survive the lunar night.

## Industry implication

A successful test would be a modest technology milestone for lunar surface operations, demonstrating use of radioisotope heating to survive lunar night and increasing Firefly's role as a lunar mission integrator. It could accelerate adoption of compact radioisotope thermal/heat sources for commercial and scientific landers.

## Key facts

- Firefly Aerospace will fly a payload from Zeno Power on an upcoming lunar lander mission.
- The payload is a radioisotope heating unit intended to test a radioisotope heat source.
- The test aims to enable future missions to survive the lunar night.

## Risks

- The summary does not specify a flight date or mission partner, so schedule and manifest risk is uncertain.
- Radioisotope payloads face regulatory, safety and launch-integration constraints that could delay or limit flight opportunities.
- As a technology test, the outcome is uncertain and may not immediately translate into widespread operational use.
