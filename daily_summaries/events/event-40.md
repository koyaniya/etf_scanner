# Rocket Lab to open Alaska launch site under $266 million Space Force deal

Rocket Lab will open a new Kodiak, Alaska launch facility under a $266 million contract with the U.S. Space Force; the facility will support up to 18 suborbital missions for military testing.

## Industry implication

This contract expands Rocket Lab's presence in U.S. national-security launch support and increases dedicated capacity for suborbital military testing, strengthening the commercial launch sector's role in defense and potentially accelerating future government launch service awards.

## Key facts

- Rocket Lab will open a new launch facility in Kodiak, Alaska.
- The work is under a $266 million deal with the U.S. Space Force.
- The Kodiak facility will support up to 18 suborbital missions for military testing.
