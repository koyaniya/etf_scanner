# Space Force awards All Points $250 million to expand satellite processing at Vandenberg

The U.S. Space Force awarded All Points Logistics a $250 million contract under a Commercial Solutions Opening to add satellite-processing facilities at Vandenberg where satellites can be inspected, tested, fueled and prepared for integration with launch vehicles; All Points Logistics is the third company selected under that Commercial Solutions Opening.

## Industry implication

The award signals increased investment by the Space Force in commercial satellite-processing capacity at Vandenberg, which could raise launch integration throughput and broaden the pool of private providers supporting national security and other government launches. It may encourage additional private investment in ground-side infrastructure and competition for future government facility modernization work.

## Key facts

- The Space Force awarded All Points Logistics $250 million.
- The award is under a Commercial Solutions Opening.
- All Points Logistics is the third company selected under that Commercial Solutions Opening.
- The contract is to add facilities at Vandenberg where satellites can be inspected, tested, fueled and prepared for integration with launch vehicles.
