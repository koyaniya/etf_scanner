# Aerospace demonstrates DiskSats in low-Earth orbit

The Aerospace Corp. is conducting on-orbit demonstrations of pancake-shaped "DiskSats" in low-Earth orbit after those satellites were launched in December on a Rocket Lab Electron, representing a successful demonstration of the DiskSat form factor.

## Industry implication

If the on-orbit demonstrations validate the DiskSat concept, it could encourage adoption of an alternative smallsat form factor and influence satellite design and manufacturing approaches. That in turn could modestly increase demand for responsive small-launch services (e.g., Rocket Lab's Electron) and spur follow-on engineering and commercial activity, though broader impact depends on demonstrated performance and scalability.
