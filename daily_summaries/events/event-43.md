# NASA Sets Coverage for Roman Space Telescope Launch from Florida

NASA and SpaceX are targeting the launch of the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket no earlier than 7:26 a.m. EDT on Sunday, Aug. 30; NASA has set coverage plans for the prelaunch and launch activities.

## Industry implication

A scheduled launch of a flagship NASA observatory on a Falcon Heavy represents near-term business for SpaceX's heavy-launch capability and maintains Falcon Heavy's relevance for major government science missions. Successful execution would reinforce SpaceX's role in providing launch services for high-profile government science payloads.

## Key facts

- NASA has set coverage plans for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope prelaunch and launch activities.
- NASA and SpaceX are targeting Roman's liftoff no earlier than 7:26 a.m. EDT Sunday, Aug. 30.
- The mission will launch aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket.
- The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is described as NASA's next-generation observatory designed to explore major questions including dark energy.

## Risks

- Launch date is a target ('no earlier than') and could slip due to technical, range, or weather constraints.
- Prelaunch issues with the vehicle or payload could delay or scrub the launch.
- Coverage plans do not guarantee launch execution; schedule changes are common for complex missions.
