# NASA’s B777 Gets New Coat of Paint

NASA’s Boeing 777 was photographed with a new paint job on Aug. 11, 2026; the aircraft was acquired to replace and extend the capabilities of NASA’s DC-8 (retired 2024) and is set to begin operations from NASA’s Langley Research Center in Hampton, Virginia in 2027.

## Industry implication

The photographed repaint and planned entry into service indicate NASA is transitioning to the B777 platform to support airborne research, which should extend NASA’s airborne science capabilities in the near term. For Boeing, the presence of a Boeing 777 in NASA service is a positive visibility event though it is unlikely to materially affect Boeing’s commercial business by itself.

## Key facts

- A photo dated Aug. 11, 2026 shows NASA’s Boeing 777 with a new paint job.
- The B777 was acquired to replace and extend the capabilities of the NASA DC-8, which was retired in 2024.
- The B777 is set to begin operations from NASA’s Langley Research Center in Hampton, Virginia in 2027.
