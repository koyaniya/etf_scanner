# Lunar Occultation of Venus

A daytime lunar occultation of Venus occurred (the Moon passed directly in front of Venus), visible across the United States and parts of Canada on Wednesday, June 17.

## Industry implication

No direct commercial or operational impact identified; primarily an observational/public outreach event of interest to astronomers and the public.

## Key facts

- The article reports a daytime lunar occultation of Venus.
- The event was visible across the United States and parts of Canada.
- The summary states the occurrence happened on "Wednesday, June 17."
- The Moon moved directly in front of Venus, briefly hiding it from sight.
