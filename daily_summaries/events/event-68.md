# Satrec Initiative to build 10-centimeter-class optical satellites for Hanwha constellation

Satrec Initiative will build 10-centimeter-class optical satellites to provide high-resolution Earth imagery for a Hanwha Systems synthetic-aperture-radar constellation announced in July; Satrec Initiative is working with its parent Hanwha Systems on the effort.

## Industry implication

Adding 10-cm-class optical sensors to a SAR constellation creates a multi-sensor capability that could improve the combined utility of Hanwha’s constellation for commercial and government imagery customers. For the industry, this reflects continued demand for higher-resolution optical imaging and a trend toward integrating optical and radar data in constellation architectures.

## Key facts

- Satrec Initiative is working with parent company Hanwha Systems to provide high-resolution Earth imagery for a synthetic-aperture-radar constellation announced in July.
- Satrec Initiative will build 10-centimeter-class optical satellites for the Hanwha constellation.

## Risks

- No event date or schedule provided — timelines for design, manufacture and deployment are unclear.
- Integration risks between optical satellites and the SAR constellation (data fusion, tasking, operations) could affect delivered capabilities.
- Competition from other high-resolution Earth-observation satellite providers may limit commercial uptake or pricing.
