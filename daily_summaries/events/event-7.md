# Lynk and Omnispace form Elveo Mobile to advance D2D services

Lynk Global completed its merger with Omnispace, rebranding as Elveo Mobile and acquiring globally coordinated S-band spectrum to expand direct-to-device satellite services beyond basic messaging.

## Industry implication

The merger and rebrand combine messaging-focused D2D capabilities with coordinated S-band spectrum, potentially enabling more advanced direct-to-device services and accelerating commercial deployments. This could intensify competition in the D2D market and put pressure on spectrum coordination and regulatory frameworks as services scale.

## Key facts

- Lynk Global has completed its merger with Omnispace.
- The merged company rebranded as Elveo Mobile.
- The company added globally coordinated S-band spectrum.
- The stated aim is to expand direct-to-device satellite services beyond basic messaging.

## Risks

- Need for regulatory approvals or coordination to use S-band spectrum in multiple jurisdictions.
- Technical and operational challenges integrating the merged companies' systems and services.
- Market adoption risk for expanded D2D services beyond messaging.
- Potential delays or constraints in deploying the satellite or network capacity required to deliver expanded services.
