# NASA Shares SpaceX Crew-14 Assignments for Space Station Mission

NASA announced crew assignments for the SpaceX Crew-14 mission: four crewmembers from three agencies are slated to launch to the ISS no earlier than spring 2027; named crew include NASA astronauts Kayla Barron (commander) and Chris Birch (pilot), JAXA astronaut Makoto Suwa, and a Roscosmos cosmonaut.

## Industry implication

The announcement confirms continued reliance on SpaceX crewed launch services for NASA's ISS rotations and underscores ongoing international crew collaboration for long-duration science missions. It helps SpaceX's manifested crew-flight pipeline and provides schedule visibility for ISS operations and research planning.

## Key facts

- NASA shared crew assignments for the SpaceX Crew-14 mission.
- Four crew members from three space agencies will launch to the International Space Station no earlier than spring 2027 for a long-duration science expedition.
- NASA astronauts Kayla Barron and Chris Birch will serve as spacecraft commander and pilot.
- JAXA astronaut Makoto Suwa is included on the crew.
- A Roscosmos cosmonaut is also included (name not provided in the summary).

## Risks

- Planned launch timing ('no earlier than spring 2027') could slip due to technical, scheduling, or programmatic issues.
- Crew assignments are subject to change before launch for medical, operational, or diplomatic reasons.
- International cooperation elements (involving Roscosmos and JAXA) could be affected by geopolitical or policy developments, potentially affecting mission composition or timing.
