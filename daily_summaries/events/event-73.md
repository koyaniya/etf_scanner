# NASA to Study Human Health, Performance During Crew-13 Mission

NASA announced that astronauts on the SpaceX Crew-13 mission to the International Space Station will support a series of biomedical and human performance investigations, including a new collaborative study of how spaceflight affects blood flow and clotting, plus tests of manual piloting skills and evaluations of methods to counter vision and brain changes.

## Industry implication

The announcement reinforces SpaceX's continued role as a provider of crewed access to the ISS and the vehicle of choice for NASA human-health research flights; it highlights ongoing use of commercial crew missions to enable biomedical investigations, which can bolster demand for future crewed missions and related services.

## Key facts

- NASA will conduct biomedical and human performance investigations during the SpaceX Crew-13 mission aboard the International Space Station.
- The investigations include a new collaborative effort to study how spaceflight affects blood flow and clotting.
- NASA will also test crew members' manual piloting skills and evaluate methods to counter vision and brain changes during the mission.
