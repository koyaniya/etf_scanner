# NASA to Study Human Health, Performance During Crew-13 Mission

NASA announced that astronauts on the SpaceX Crew-13 mission to the International Space Station will support a series of biomedical and human performance investigations, including a collaborative study of how spaceflight affects blood flow and clotting, tests of manual piloting skills, and evaluations of methods to counter vision and brain changes. The planned Crew-13 flight will carry four crewmembers and its on-orbit activities include research, technology demonstrations, and maintenance aboard the ISS.

## Industry implication

The announcement reinforces SpaceX's continued role as a provider of crewed access to the ISS and the vehicle of choice for NASA human-health research flights; using commercial crew missions to enable biomedical investigations supports ongoing NASA research objectives and can bolster demand for future crewed missions, technology demonstrations, and related services.
