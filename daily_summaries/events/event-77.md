# NASA Awards Orbital Safety Analysis Support Services Contract

NASA awarded an indefinite-delivery contract to Omitron Inc. for orbital safety analysis support services, valued at approximately $23.5 million over a five-year period, to provide conjunction assessment screening for all NASA uncrewed spacecraft.

## Industry implication

The award signals continued NASA investment in external conjunction-assessment and orbital safety capabilities. That may sustain or grow demand for commercial providers of collision-avoidance and orbital-debris services and reinforces NASA's emphasis on protecting flight assets and crew safety over the next several years.

## Key facts

- NASA awarded a contract to Omitron Inc. for orbital safety analysis services.
- The contract is an indefinite-delivery contract valued at approximately $23.5 million over a five-year period of performance.
- The work entails conjunction assessment screening services for all NASA uncrewed spacecraft.
- The contract is described as providing critical protection of NASA spaceflight assets and astronaut lives.

## Risks

- Contract performance risk if Omitron cannot meet NASA's conjunction-assessment needs or timelines.
- Funding or scope changes to NASA programs over the five-year period could alter the contract's effective value or activity level.
- Dependence on a single awarded contractor for a portfolio of conjunction-assessment services could create operational concentration risk if issues arise.
