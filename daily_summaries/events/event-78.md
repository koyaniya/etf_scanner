# NASA Sets Crew-13 Launch, Docking Coverage

NASA announced live coverage and streaming of prelaunch, launch, and docking activities for SpaceX's Crew-13 mission to the International Space Station, and stated Crew-13 is scheduled to launch at 11:10 a.m. EDT on Thursday, Oct. 1 from Space Launch Complex 40.

## Industry implication

This is a near-term operational announcement for a crewed launch that reinforces ongoing commercial crew operations with SpaceX and provides public visibility; it is routine operational news with limited broader industry impact beyond scheduled launch activity and publicity.

## Key facts

- NASA will provide live coverage of prelaunch, launch, and docking activities for the agency’s SpaceX Crew-13 mission to the International Space Station.
- Live coverage will stream through a variety of platforms (NASA stated readers can watch via NASA Live).
- Crew-13 is scheduled to launch at 11:10 a.m. EDT, Thursday, Oct. 1, from Space Launch Complex 40.

## Risks

- Scheduled launch date/time may change due to technical issues, range conflicts, or weather (common for launch operations).
- Announcement of coverage does not indicate the outcome of the launch or docking; mission could be delayed or aborted.
- Operational or logistical issues at the launch site or with the vehicle could impact the schedule.
