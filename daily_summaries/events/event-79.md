# Crew-13 Rocket and Spacecraft at Launch Pad

A SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket and Dragon spacecraft were photographed vertical at Space Launch Complex 40, Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, on Sept. 27, 2026, ahead of NASA’s SpaceX Crew-13 mission.

## Industry implication

The vehicle being at the pad indicates Crew-13 was at an advanced pre-launch stage on Sept. 27, 2026, supporting near-term launch cadence for SpaceX’s crewed operations with NASA. This is a short-term operational milestone rather than a strategic or commercial contract announcement.

## Key facts

- A SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket and Dragon spacecraft were vertical at the launch pad of Space Launch Complex 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station (photo dated Sept. 27, 2026).
- The image and caption refer to NASA’s SpaceX Crew-13 mission.
- Named crew members in the summary include NASA astronauts Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney and CSA astronaut Joshua Kutryk; the summary also references a Roscosmos cosmonaut Sergey.

## Risks

- Pad status is a pre-launch snapshot; launches can be delayed or scrubbed for technical, weather, or programmatic reasons.
- Photographing a vehicle at the pad does not indicate launch success; subsequent integrated testing or countdown issues could change outcomes.
- Inclusion of international crew agencies (NASA, CSA, Roscosmos) can be subject to scheduling or diplomatic impacts beyond the vehicle’s readiness.
