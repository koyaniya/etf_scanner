# Heading Home: NASA’s SpaceX Crew-12 Concludes Station Science Mission

NASA’s SpaceX Crew-12 increment is concluding and the four-person crew (NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Jack Hathaway, ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Andrey Fedyaev) are scheduled to undock and return from the International Space Station in early October 2026. NASA and SpaceX are targeting no earlier than 8:05 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026, for undocking, and NASA intends to stream the crew’s return and splashdown live. An earlier report described the return as occurring in "early October 2026," creating a discrepancy on the precise planned date.

## Industry implication

A routine, successful Crew-12 undocking and splashdown would reinforce confidence in commercial crew transport (SpaceX Dragon) and continued ISS utilization for research. The planned live stream also increases public visibility for commercial crew operations. Immediate commercial or financial impacts are limited; operational continuity and public trust are the primary implications.

## Key facts

- NASA’s SpaceX Crew-12 mission is concluding and the crew is scheduled to return from the International Space Station after completing station research.
- Crew members are NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Jack Hathaway, ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Andrey Fedyaev.
- NASA and SpaceX are targeting no earlier than 8:05 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026, for undocking of the Crew-12 spacecraft from the ISS.
- NASA intends to stream the Crew-12 return and splashdown live.
- One source described the return as occurring in "early October 2026," without specifying a date.

## Risks

- Undocking and splashdown schedules are subject to change due to weather at recovery zones.
- In-flight or ground technical issues could delay or alter undocking/splashdown timing.
- There is a contradiction in the reported event date (an "early October" description and a specific Oct. 7 target); the exact landing/undocking date/time may therefore be uncertain.
