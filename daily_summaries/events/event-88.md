# NASA Testing Aims at Supercooled Large Droplet Aviation Safety

NASA is conducting testing and upgrading its test facilities to address 'supercooled large droplet' (SLD) icing, a hazardous condition that occurs when aircraft encounter unusually large, very cold water droplets in clouds.
