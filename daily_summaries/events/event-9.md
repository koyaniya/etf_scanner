# Intuitive Machines wins contract for three GEO satellites

Intuitive Machines Inc announced on Aug. 13 that it won a $600 million contract from an undisclosed customer to build three geostationary-orbit communications satellites.

## Industry implication

The contract represents a significant commercial win for Intuitive Machines and signals the company's expansion into building geostationary communications satellites, with potential revenue and capability growth in satellite manufacturing and satellite communications. The undisclosed customer leaves uncertainty about whether the deal affects government vs. commercial market dynamics and about downstream aspects such as launch procurement and operations.

## Key facts

- Intuitive Machines announced Aug. 13 that it won a $600 million contract.
- The contract is for three geostationary orbit (GEO) communications satellites.
- The customer for the contract was not disclosed in the announcement.
- The information was reported by SpaceNews.

## Risks

- Execution risk: building three GEO communications satellites involves technical and schedule risk that could affect delivery and revenue recognition.
- Customer undisclosed: lack of customer details creates uncertainty about payment terms, launch arrangements, and long-term operational commitments.
- Supply-chain and cost risk: satellite manufacturing can face component shortages or cost overruns that could reduce margin on the contract.
- Competition and market risk: other satellite manufacturers and prime contractors could respond competitively in the GEO communications market.
