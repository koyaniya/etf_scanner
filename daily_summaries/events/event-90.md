# NASA to Cover Northrop Grumman CRS-24 Spacecraft Departure

Northrop Grumman’s Cygnus XL spacecraft (CRS-24) is scheduled to undock and depart the International Space Station on Friday, Oct. 9; NASA will provide live coverage of the undocking and departure beginning at 12:30.

## Industry implication

Routine undocking/departure signals successful completion of Northrop Grumman’s CRS-24 cargo delivery under NASA’s Commercial Resupply Services program, supporting ongoing ISS operations and demonstrating continued fulfillment of the government resupply contract.

## Key facts

- Northrop Grumman’s Cygnus XL spacecraft delivered more than 11,000 pounds of supplies, science experiments, and other cargo to the International Space Station, per the article summary.
- The Cygnus XL spacecraft is scheduled to depart (undock from) the ISS on Friday, Oct. 9 as part of the Northrop Grumman CRS-24 mission.
- NASA will provide live coverage of the undocking and departure beginning at 12:30 (local time as stated in the summary).
