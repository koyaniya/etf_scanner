# NASA Advances LISA Mission Contributions With New Test Telescope

NASA has advanced development of an all-glass telescope for the LISA mission by moving forward with an Engineering Test Unit that L3Harris Technologies will design, assemble, and integrate.

## Industry implication

This advances optical-hardware development for the LISA gravitational-wave observatory and reinforces L3Harris's role as a supplier for precision space optics; it could increase L3Harris's visibility for future government space instrumentation work and help de-risk later LISA hardware development stages.

## Key facts

- NASA is developing a new all-glass telescope for the LISA (Laser Interferometer Space Antenna) mission.
- L3Harris Technologies will design, assemble, and integrate the new telescope.
- The hardware is referred to as the Engineering Test Unit.
- The article states NASA has taken the next step in the process of developing this telescope.

## Risks

- Technical development risk for a novel all-glass telescope could affect schedule or performance.
- Article summary provides limited detail on contract scope, schedule, and value, creating uncertainty about program scale and timeline.
- Concentrating design/assembly/integration with a single contractor could concentrate execution and supply-chain risk.
