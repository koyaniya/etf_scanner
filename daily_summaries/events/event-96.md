# NASA’s SPHEREx Telescope Sees Menagerie of Brown Dwarfs

NASA’s SPHEREx space telescope produced new scientific findings about brown dwarfs; those results were published in The Astrophysical Journal and report that brown dwarfs have chemically rich atmospheres.

## Industry implication

The published SPHEREx results validate the mission's scientific return and may drive follow-up observations, increase demand for spectroscopic datasets, and inform models of substellar atmospheres; implications are primarily scientific rather than commercial but could influence future mission planning and instrument requirements.

## Key facts

- NASA’s SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization, and Ices Explorer) space telescope is the source of the findings.
- New findings from SPHEREx about brown dwarfs were published in The Astrophysical Journal.
- The findings indicate brown dwarfs have chemically rich atmospheres.
