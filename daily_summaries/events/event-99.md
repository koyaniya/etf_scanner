# NASA Demonstrates Next-Generation Heat Shield Technologies

When Northrop Grumman Corp’s 24th cargo resupply mission undocked from the International Space Station, it carried 12 small experimental capsules that NASA is using to demonstrate next‑generation heat shield technologies intended to protect astronauts returning from Moon and Mars missions.

## Industry implication

If the experiments succeed, these demonstrations could validate new reentry/heat‑shield approaches and accelerate development of crewed return systems for lunar and Mars missions, benefiting NASA mission planning and contractors involved in reentry thermal protection.

## Key facts

- Northrop Grumman 24th cargo resupply mission undocked from the International Space Station (per the summary).
- The mission carried a fleet of 12 small, experimental capsules.
- NASA is using those capsules to test next‑generation heat shield technologies aimed at protecting astronauts returning from the Moon and Mars.
- The demonstration was described as being performed in an unusual way — “by taking out the trash” — implying use of departure/disposal from the ISS to host the experiments.
